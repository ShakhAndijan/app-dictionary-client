# 1 | Present perfect vs past simple | Qoida: Tugagan va aniq vaqtga bog'langan ishlarda (yesterday, in 2019, ago, last week) past simple ishlatiladi: I saw her yesterday. Hozirgacha davom etayotgan yoki hozirga ta'siri bor ishlarda present perfect keladi (already, yet, just, ever, never, this week, since, for): I have known her since 2015. O'zbek tilida ikkalasi ham o'tgan zamon bilan ifodalanadi, shuning uchun vaqt so'ziga qarab tanlang. | Aniq o'tgan vaqt (ago, last, in 2019) bo'lsa past simple, since/for/already/ever bo'lsa present perfect.
