# 5 | Used to, would, be used to, get used to | Qoida: "used to + fe'l" o'tmishdagi odat yoki holatni bildiradi (I used to live in Rome); "would" faqat takrorlanuvchi harakatlar uchun ishlatiladi, holat fe'llari bilan emas. "be used to" va "get used to"dan keyin -ing shakli keladi: I am used to waking up early (ko'nikkanman), I'm getting used to it (ko'nikyapman). "I am used to live" noto'g'ri. | Odat uchun "used to" + fe'l, ko'nikish uchun "be/get used to" + -ing.

# 7 | Conditionals 0, 1 and 2 with unless, as long as, provided, in case | Qoida: if gapida "will" ishlatilmaydi: If it rains, we will stay home (not "if it will rain").
