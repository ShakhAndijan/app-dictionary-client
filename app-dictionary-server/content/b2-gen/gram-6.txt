# 24 | Linking words and contrast | Qoida: although, though va even though dan keyin to'liq gap keladi (Although it rained, we went out), despite va in spite of dan keyin esa ot yoki -ing (Despite the rain; in spite of being tired); "despite of" xato. Because + gap, because of + ot. So that va in order to maqsadni bildiradi, in case ehtimol bo'lgan holatga tayyorgarlikni, if esa shartni. Otherwise va unless "aks holda / agar ...masa" ma'nosida; as a result va therefore natijani bildiradi.
