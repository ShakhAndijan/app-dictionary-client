# 11 | Past Continuous: I was working | O'tgan zamonda ma'lum paytda davom etayotgan ish: was / were + fe'l-ing. I was reading at nine. They were playing. | was / were + fe'l-ing: o'tgan paytda davom etayotgan ish.
I [was reading] a book at nine o'clock. | read, was read, were reading ~ read
They [were playing] football at five. | was playing, played, are playing ~ play
She [was cooking] when I called. | cooked, were cooking, is cooking ~ cook
We [were watching] TV all evening. | was watching, watched, watching ~ watch
It [was raining] when we left. | were raining, rained, is raining ~ rain
What [were] you doing at eight last night? | was, did, are
[Was] he sleeping at ten? | Were, Did, Is
I [wasn't listening] to the teacher. | weren't listening, didn't listening, wasn't listen ~ listen
They [weren't working] yesterday afternoon. | wasn't working, didn't working, aren't working ~ work
Where [was] she going when you saw her? | were, did, is
The children [were sleeping] at midnight. | was sleeping, slept, sleeping ~ sleep
He [was driving] to work at seven. | were driving, drove, is driving ~ drive

# 12 | Past Simple va Past Continuous (when, while) | Uzoq davom etgan ish: Past Continuous. Uni to'xtatgan qisqa voqea: Past Simple. I was cooking when the phone rang. While bilan davom etgan ish keladi. | Davom etgan ish: was / were + -ing. Qisqa voqea: Past Simple.
I was cooking when the phone [rang]. | was ringing, rings ~ ring
She was walking when she [saw] an accident. | was seeing, sees ~ see
While I [was studying], my brother came in. | studied, am studying ~ study
They were playing when it [started] to rain. | was starting, starts ~ start
He [fell] while he was running. | was falling, falls ~ fall
We were having dinner when the lights [went] out. | were going, go ~ go
While she [was reading], I was cooking. | read, were reading ~ read
I [met] my wife when I was working in Paris. | was meeting, meet ~ meet

He was sleeping when I [came] home. | was coming, come ~ come
The teacher [was explaining] when the bell rang. | explained, were explaining ~ explain
I [was crossing] the street when I saw him. | crossed, cross ~ cross

# 14 | Present Perfect: I have done | Hozir bilan bog'liq tugagan ish: have / has + fe'lning 3-shakli. I have lost my key (hozir yo'q). Aniq vaqt aytilmaydi. | have / has + 3-shakl: natija hozir bilan bog'liq.

# 18 | Present Perfect va Past Simple | Aniq o'tgan vaqt aytilsa (yesterday, in 2010, last week) - Past Simple. Vaqt aytilmasa yoki hozirgacha - Present Perfect. | yesterday, ago, last, in 2010: Past Simple. ever, never, just, yet, since, for: Present Perfect.

# 21 | will | Qaror va bashorat: will + fe'l (I will help you; It will rain tomorrow). Inkor: won't. Savol: Will you...? Shu zahoti qilingan qaror uchun ham will. | will + asosiy fe'l: hamma shaxs uchun bir xil, fe'lga -s qo'shilmaydi.

# 48 | Sifat va ravish: quick / quickly | Sifat otni tasvirlaydi (a quick answer). Ravish fe'lni tasvirlaydi, odatda -ly bilan (He answered quickly). Istisno: good - well, fast - fast, hard - hard. | Sifat + ot: quick answer. Fe'l + ravish: answered quickly.
